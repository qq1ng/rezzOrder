== Mechanics ==
* The [[range|radius]] for [[revive| revival]] underwater is 240 units around the guardian.
* The skill [[revival|revives]] other allied characters from the [[Downed]] state only, and not the [[Defeated]] state. Defeated [[NPC]] allies can be resurrected.
* The skill may fail to revive a downed ally if they are [[poisoned]] and below a certain health threshold.
* If this skill successfully revives an ally it will activate {{item icon|Relic of Mercy}} if it is equipped.
