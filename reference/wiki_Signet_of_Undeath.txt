== Notes ==
* As revival is affected by [[healing]] modifiers, a [[poisoned]] player with less than 33% health will not be fully revived.
* Activating this skill costs additional 50% of the base [[health]] and the health gained from the base [[vitality]], which is 9,606 health at level 80.
* [[Barrier]] will reduce the health lost.
* The cost is not affected by effects that reduce maximum health, such as [[Blight]] or [[Kryptis Essence]]/[[Calibrated Essence]].
