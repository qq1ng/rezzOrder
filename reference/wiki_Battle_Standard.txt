== Mechanics ==
;Revive and finisher
* Players can be revived from [[downed]] state only, most [[NPC]]s can be revived from defeat.
* Because the banner has a short travel time when it falls from the air after cast completion, it can also revive the caster if timed perfectly.
* The revive technically heals players for 100% of their total down state health and 100% of their normal health.
** Through debuffs (such as [[Poisoned]] and/or [[Agony]]), the banner's revival capabilities can be negated to the point that the affected player will not revive at all, and only regain a portion of their downed health. 
** This is a common issue in fractals, as higher level fractals can cause players to have multiple stacks of agony, thus nullifying the effects completely.
* The banner doesn't finish foes if they are invisible.

; Boons
* The first application happens when the banner is summoned.
* The banner will then reapply the [[boon]]s every 3 seconds for its duration.
* This means a total of 6 applications, for a total of 36 seconds of {{fury}} and 72 seconds of {{swiftness}}. 12 stacks of {{might}} will be applied, with varying total stacks over the duration.
