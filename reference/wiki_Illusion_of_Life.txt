== Mechanics ==
* Functionally, the skill is a direct revive with an additional effect: if no enemy is killed by the ally, they are dealt 100% of maximum health as damage (unaffected by damage reductions and [[barrier]]) when the effect ends.
** Direct revive means that the resurrection is applied independent of other effects (e.g. poison).
** If an ally were to go into downstate while under this effect before it times out, they will become [[defeated]] instead.
** If an ally is under the effect of {{effect|Defiant Stance (effect)}} or {{effect|Infuse Light (effect)}} as the timer ends, they will be healed instead of going into [[downed]] state.
** Mounting up before the timer ends will prevent going into downed state and just de-mount you instead.
** Being downed from this damage will incur further [[downed penalty]].
*** If at maximum [[downed penalty]] the ally will become [[defeated]] instead.
